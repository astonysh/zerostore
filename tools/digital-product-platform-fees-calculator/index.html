Gumroad vs Lemon Squeezy vs Payhip Fees

The headline rate is not what you pay. Enter your price and volume and see what lands in your account on each platform — including the processing fees Gumroad charges on top, and the surcharges that only apply to some buyers.

Fee rates checked against each platform's published pricing, October 2026.

Short answer: Gumroad takes 10% + $0.50 on direct sales plus card processing (about 2.9% + $0.30) — roughly 13% + $0.80 all in — and 30% flat on Discover sales. Lemon Squeezy takes 5% + $0.50 all in, plus 1.5% for international cards or PayPal. Payhip takes 5% on the free plan, 2% on Plus ($29/mo) or 0% on Pro ($99/mo), and you pay your own Stripe or PayPal fees on top. All three are one-time-fee, no-monthly-cost on their entry plans — except Payhip Plus and Pro.

Three things the headline rates hide

1. The flat fee is a percentage that grows as your price falls

$0.50 is 0.5% of a $100 product and 10% of a $5 one. On a $5 sale Gumroad's flat fee alone matches its percentage fee, and Lemon Squeezy's is double it. Cheap products are structurally disadvantaged on every platform here — the fix is price, bundling, or both, not platform shopping.

2. Processing is only "included" where the platform says so

Lemon Squeezy's 5% + $0.50 is the whole cost. Gumroad's 10% + $0.50 is not: card processing is billed separately, which is why the calculator above applies it and why Gumroad usually shows up third on a $29 product. Payhip is in the same position as Gumroad — the platform fee is not the whole story.

3. Marketplace sales are a different product

Gumroad's Discover charges 30% flat but includes processing and can bring buyers you would not otherwise have. Treat it as a customer-acquisition cost, not a fee: if a Discover sale costs 30% but is a customer you could not reach, it may still be the cheapest sale you make that month. Nothing here promises sales — Discover lists a product only after the account has real sales and passes review.

Which should you pick?

  • Starting out, own traffic only: Lemon Squeezy, unless you are on Payhip Pro or expect a high-volume Gumroad month.
  • Cheap product under about $10: raise the price or bundle. No platform fixes a flat fee that is 10% of your price.
  • Want a marketplace to find buyers: Gumroad, and accept the 30% on those sales.
  • Already have a Stripe account and volume: Payhip Pro's 0% platform fee becomes interesting once 2% of your revenue exceeds $99 a month — that is $4,950/month in sales on the Plus plan.
  • Selling on Etsy already: adding a digital download to an existing Etsy shop costs you nothing extra to try.

Questions sellers actually ask

Which is cheapest in 2026?

On a $29 product with own traffic, Lemon Squeezy at 5% + $0.50 is usually lowest. Payhip Pro beats it above about $4,950/month in sales. Gumroad's direct rate is the highest of the three because processing is charged on top.

Does Gumroad charge processing on top of 10%?

Yes, on direct sales. Discover sales are 30% flat with processing included.

What is a merchant of record?

The party legally selling to the buyer, responsible for collecting and remitting sales tax and VAT. Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy are merchants of record; Payhip is not, though it handles EU/UK VAT on digital goods.

Can I build my own storefront instead?

Yes, and you can still use any of these three as the checkout — which is exactly what the ZeroStore kit does. A static storefront on GitHub Pages costs nothing to host and you keep the platform you prefer.

Do I need license keys?

Only if you are selling software. Gumroad has a verification API — see the JavaScript guide — and Lemon Squeezy and Payhip both issue keys too.
