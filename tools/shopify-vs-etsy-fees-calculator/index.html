Shopify vs Etsy Fees Calculator

Etsy charges nothing monthly and more per sale. Shopify charges monthly and less per sale. Enter your numbers and see which side of that trade you are on — and the exact order volume where it flips.

Fee rates checked against Etsy's and Shopify's published pricing, October 2026. US rates.

Short answer: Etsy costs roughly 9.7% + $0.45 per order and no monthly fee. Shopify Basic costs $39/month plus 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. At a $50 average order, Etsy's per-sale premium is about $3.45, so Shopify's monthly fee pays for itself at roughly 11 orders a month. Below that, Etsy is cheaper; above it, Shopify is — before you count the traffic Etsy brings and the traffic Shopify does not.

What each number includes

Etsy side

  • $0.20 listing fee per sale (auto-renew)
  • $0.20 × listings ÷ 4 for listings that renew every four months without selling
  • 6.5% transaction fee on the order total, shipping included
  • 3% + $0.25 payment processing (US)
  • Offsite Ads: 15% or 12% on the share of orders you say come from ads, capped at $100 per order

Excludes any Etsy Ads (cost-per-click) spend, which is a marketing line, not a platform fee.

Shopify side

  • Monthly plan fee ($39 / $105 / $399, monthly billing)
  • Card rate: 2.9% / 2.7% / 2.5% + $0.30
  • Third-party gateway surcharge — 2% / 1% / 0.6% — only if you do not use Shopify Payments
  • Whatever you enter as other monthly costs

Excludes the cost of getting traffic. Etsy supplies demand; a Shopify store supplies a checkout.

The comparison everyone forgets: Etsy's fees are buying you access to shoppers searching for what you sell. Shopify's fees buy you a checkout and a website — not visitors. A store with no traffic costs $39 a month and sells nothing. Put that in the "other monthly costs" box as ad spend if you want a truer picture.

Costs the fee tables leave out

  • Etsy: listings that never sell still renew. Every listing costs $0.20 every four months whether or not anyone buys it. 100 live listings is about $5 a month, sold or not.
  • Etsy: Offsite Ads are not optional once you pass $10,000. Shops below the threshold can opt out; above it, participation is required for the life of the shop.
  • Shopify: apps add up faster than the plan fee. Reviews, email, bundles, subscriptions — $20–$60 a month is ordinary, and most apps bill by store not by order.
  • Shopify: annual billing cuts the plan fee ($29/month effective on Basic) at the cost of paying upfront.
  • Both: refunds, chargebacks and remakes. Neither platform's fee table is where those show up. They show up in your profit sheet.

Questions sellers actually ask

Is Shopify cheaper than Etsy?

Only above a certain volume. At a $50 average order, Shopify Basic's $39 monthly fee is worth about 11 Etsy orders. Below roughly that many orders a month, Etsy is cheaper.

Does Shopify charge a transaction fee?

Not with Shopify Payments — you pay only the card rate. The 2% surcharge on Basic applies only if you use a different gateway.

Should I leave Etsy for Shopify?

Only if you already have, or can buy, your own traffic. Moving an Etsy shop to Shopify usually means trading fees for marketing spend; the fee saving is often smaller than the traffic loss. Many sellers run both.

Which platform is better for handmade?

Etsy brings buyers who are already shopping handmade; Shopify gives you more control and a better margin on repeat customers. Neither guarantees sales — that depends on your product and your marketing.
