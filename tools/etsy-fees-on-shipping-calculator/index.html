Is the Etsy Fee Charged on Shipping?

Yes — and the size of that bite surprises people. Put a number on it below, then see why "free shipping" does not save you a cent in Etsy fees.

Fee rates checked against Etsy's published policy, October 2026.

Short answer: Yes. Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee is charged on the item price plus the shipping and gift wrap you charge the buyer — not on the item alone. Payment processing (US: 3% + $0.25) is charged on the same order total. On a $6.00 shipping charge a US seller pays about $0.57 to Etsy, roughly 9.5%. Etsy does not charge these fees on sales tax it collects for you.

Charging shipping vs. "free shipping" — the same bill

This is the part most sellers get wrong. Because Etsy takes its percentage of the order total, moving the shipping cost into the item price changes nothing about what Etsy charges — as long as the customer pays the same amount overall.

Identical. The only way free shipping reduces your Etsy bill is if the customer pays less in total — for example, you absorb part of the postage instead of adding all of it to the price. That is a margin decision, not a fee-saving one.

What free shipping does change: conversion and how your listing is presented. Etsy has run free-shipping programmes in the US market, and buyers filter on it. Test it on your own listings with the numbers above before you decide — and remember that "free" shipping is not free, it is prepaid.

Where shipping costs you beyond the fee

  • You pay postage on the fee-inclusive amount you never received. If you charge $6 and postage is $6, you are even on postage — but Etsy still took $0.57 of the $6, so you are $0.57 down before materials.
  • Undercharging shipping is the most common quiet loss. Boxes, tape, padding and the label are real costs. Add them to the shipping charge or to the item price, but put them somewhere.
  • International orders can add currency conversion (2.5%) and a higher processing rate. See Etsy fees by country.
  • Offsite Ad orders stack an extra 15% or 12% on the whole total, shipping included — check that calculator if you get ad-attributed sales.

Questions sellers actually ask

Is the Etsy fee charged on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee applies to item price plus shipping and gift wrap you charge, and payment processing applies to the same order total. A $6.00 shipping charge costs a US seller about $0.57 in Etsy fees.

Does free shipping save Etsy fees?

Not if you move the shipping cost into the price. Etsy's percentage fees are the same on the same order total either way.

Are gift wrap charges fee'd?

Yes — gift wrap you charge for is part of the order total and carries the same 6.5% and processing fees.

Is Etsy's fee charged on sales tax?

No. For US sellers the transaction fee is calculated on the order amount excluding sales tax collected by Etsy.

What about a shipping label bought through Etsy?

Labels bought through Etsy are charged to your payment account at the label price; they are a cost of sale, not an Etsy fee, and they do not earn you a fee credit. Track them as a cost so your profit number is honest.
