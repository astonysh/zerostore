Etsy Fees by Country (2026)

The listing fee and the 6.5% transaction fee are the same everywhere. Payment processing and a country-specific regulatory operating fee are not. Pick your country and see the real stack — including the fee rises that landed on 22 June 2026.

Rates checked against Etsy's published policy, October 2026. Confirm your own rate in Shop Manager → Finances before you price against it.

Short answer: Etsy's $0.20 listing fee and 6.5% transaction fee apply to every seller. What changes by country is payment processing — UK 4% + £0.20, Canada 3% + C$0.25 domestically and 4% + C$0.25 internationally, Australia 3% / 4% + A$0.25, euro area 4% + €0.30 — plus a regulatory operating fee in some countries, which Etsy raised on 22 June 2026 (UK to 0.48%, France to 1.14%, Spain 0.88%, Italy 0.80%, Hungary 1.97%). Fees depend on where your shop is, not where your buyer is.

The 22 June 2026 regulatory operating fee rise

Etsy charges a regulatory operating fee in some countries to cover the cost of complying with local regulation, and passes it to sellers. On 22 June 2026 it went up in several markets — France saw the biggest jump, more than doubling from 0.47% to 1.14%.

Three things make this fee easy to miss:

  • It applies to the order total, shipping included — the same base as the 6.5% transaction fee.
  • It is tied to your shop's location, not your buyer's. A UK seller pays the UK rate on orders to anywhere; US-based sellers pay nothing.
  • There is no opt-out. It appears as its own line on your payment account.

Per order the extra is small — on a €35 order, France's increase adds about €0.23. Across a year of orders, and on a thin margin, it is worth knowing it is there. Add your country's rate in the calculator above and it is included in the total.

What is the same everywhere

  • $0.20 listing fee per item, charged in your shop currency at the equivalent amount, every time a listing sells or renews.
  • 6.5% transaction fee on the order total — item price plus the shipping and gift wrap you charge.
  • 2.5% currency conversion if your listing currency differs from your payment account currency.
  • Offsite Ads: 15%, or 12% once you have sold $10,000 USD in a rolling 365-day period, capped at $100 per order. See the Offsite Ads calculator.
VAT and GST on fees: Etsy's fees are subject to VAT for UK and EU sellers and to GST in Australia. If you are VAT-registered and can reclaim it, it is neutral; if you are not, it is a real cost and the headline processing rate understates what you pay. Questions sellers actually ask

Do Etsy fees depend on the buyer's country or mine?

Yours. Processing depends on your seller bank account's country and the regulatory operating fee on your shop's registration. The 6.5% transaction fee and the listing fee do not vary.

Why do Canada and Australia have two processing rates?

Domestic (and, for Canada, US) orders are charged the lower rate; international orders are charged a point more. Which one applies depends on where the buyer is for that specific order.

Do I pay the regulatory fee on every order?

If your shop is in a country where Etsy charges it — yes, on every order, on the full order total. There is no setting to turn it off.

Does the transaction fee change by country?

No. 6.5% is charged to sellers in every market Etsy operates in. Country differences sit in processing, the regulatory operating fee and local tax on Etsy's fees.

I sell in a currency that is not my payment currency. What happens?

Etsy converts the sale amount at the market rate and charges a 2.5% currency conversion fee on it. Tick the conversion box above to include it.
