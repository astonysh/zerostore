Etsy Offsite Ads Fee Calculator

Enter an order and see exactly what an Offsite Ad costs you — at the 15% and 12% rates, with the $100 per-order cap applied. Compare the same order with and without ad attribution.

Fee rates checked against Etsy's published policy, October 2026.

Short answer: Etsy charges 15% of the order total on a sale attributed to an Offsite Ad — dropping to 12% once your shop has made $10,000 USD or more in any consecutive 365-day period. The fee is capped at $100 per order, so it stops growing once the order passes $666.67 (at 15%) or $833.33 (at 12%). It is charged in addition to the normal listing, transaction and processing fees.

What the two rates actually mean for your pricing

Because the ad fee is a percentage of the amount the buyer pays, you cannot absorb it by "adding the fee on top" — you have to divide by what is left. To keep the same net revenue after an attributed sale:

price after an ad sale = price ÷ (1 − ad rate)

15% → ÷ 0.85 = +17.6% on your price
12% → ÷ 0.88 = +13.6% on your price

So a $45 item needs to sell for about $52.94 to net the same $45 at the 15% rate. Most sellers do not do this per order — they set one price that already covers the expected share of ad-attributed sales, or they opt out while they are eligible.

Opt-out rule: shops that have never sold $10,000 USD in a rolling 365-day period can turn Offsite Ads off in Shop Manager → Settings → Offsite Ads. Once you cross $10,000, participation is required for the lifetime of the shop and the rate falls to 12%. Etsy's own help page is the authority here — check it before you rely on either.

Questions sellers actually ask

How much is the Etsy Offsite Ads fee in 2026?

15% of the attributed order total under $10,000 USD in a rolling 365-day period, 12% at or above it. Capped at $100 USD per order.

Does the fee apply to shipping and gift wrap?

Yes. Etsy's ad fee is a percentage of the order total, which includes what you charge for shipping and gift wrap — the same base the 6.5% transaction fee uses. See the fees on shipping calculator for that side of the stack.

Is it charged on repeat orders from the same buyer?

Attribution lasts 30 days from the ad click, and a single click can lead to more than one order in that window. Each attributed order carries its own fee, each capped at $100.

What if the buyer refunds?

Etsy does not charge the Offsite Ads fee on a refunded order when the refund is processed before the fee would apply, and the standard fees are reversed per Etsy's policy. Confirm the current treatment on Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy — it is the only source that matters.

Do I also pay the 6.5% transaction fee on an ad sale?

Yes. The ad fee stacks on top of every other fee. On a 15% attributed order the combined take is often over 25% of the order total — the calculator above shows the full stack.
