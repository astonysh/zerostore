Etsy fees in 2026, explained

How much does Etsy take per sale?

US sellers pay a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on item price plus shipping, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing. Without ads that's usually 10–13% of the order. If the buyer came through an Offsite Ad, Etsy adds 15% (12% if you sold over $10k in the last 12 months), capped at $100 per order — total fees can reach ~25%.

Is the transaction fee charged on shipping?

Yes — the 6.5% applies to what you charge for shipping and gift wrap too. "Free shipping" with the cost baked into the price is charged the same.

How do I price for a target margin?

Price = (costs + fixed fees) ÷ (1 − percentage fees − target margin) − shipping charged. The calculator above solves it for you.

Do these rates apply outside the US?

Processing fees vary by country (e.g. UK, EU, Canada). Open "Advanced" above and enter the rates from Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy for your country.
